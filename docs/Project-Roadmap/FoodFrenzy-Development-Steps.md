# FoodFrenzy — Commercial Product Development Roadmap

Step 1 — Foundation & Clean Architecture
Solution structure, projects, Clean Architecture, initial setup.

Step 2 — Database & Persistence Foundation
SQL Server, EF Core, DbContext, User entity, migrations.

Step 3 — Repository & Data Access Layer
Repository pattern, interfaces, Infrastructure implementation.

Step 4 — Domain & Business Foundation
Business requirements, geography, restaurants, branches, menus, entity relationships, database foundation.

Step 5 — Identity, Authorization & Security Foundation
User identity, roles, permissions, restaurant ownership, branch assignments, data isolation, audit, soft delete, concurrency and security.

Step 6 — Customer & Account Management
Customer profile, addresses, preferences and account management.

Step 7 — Restaurant Operations
Restaurant ownership, staff, branch operations and restaurant management.

Step 8 — Menu & Catalog Management
Menus, categories, items, pricing, availability and catalog rules.

Step 9 — Cart & Ordering
Cart lifecycle, order creation, order states and business rules.

Step 10 — Payment & Financial Management
Payments, transactions, refunds, payment status and financial consistency.

Step 11 — Delivery Management
Delivery partners, assignment, tracking and delivery lifecycle.

Step 12 — Notification & Communication
Email/SMS/push notifications and reliable notification processing.

Step 13 — Promotions, Reviews & Ratings
Coupons, promotions, reviews, ratings and related business rules.

Step 14 — Administration & Operations
Admin tools, support operations, configuration and platform management.

Step 15 — Security Hardening & Audit
Advanced authorization, auditing, security policies and protection.

Step 16 — Testing & Quality Engineering
Unit tests, integration tests, API tests, business-rule testing and regression testing.

Step 17 — Performance & Scalability
Caching, database optimization, indexing, concurrency and performance testing.

Step 18 — Observability & Reliability
Logging, monitoring, health checks, metrics and failure handling.

Step 19 — API & Documentation
Swagger/OpenAPI, API versioning, developer documentation and customer documentation.

Step 20 — AWS Production Deployment
Cloud infrastructure, configuration, database deployment, CI/CD and production environment.

Step 21 — AI Services
Recommendations, intelligent search, customer assistance, analytics and other AI capabilities.

Step 22 — Production Readiness & Commercial Release
Final security review, performance review, deployment verification, documentation and release preparation.
